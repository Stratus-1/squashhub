# Club Ladder member visibility

## Goal
Put the member visibility control directly in Club Admin → Club Ladder settings, while keeping admin ladder access unchanged.

## Changes
- Add a clear “Show club ladder to members” switch at the top of Ladder & Challenge Rules.
- Reuse the existing club ladder visibility setting, so this control and the Features screen remain in sync.
- Explain that switching it off hides the ladder from members while admins can still arrange and manage it.
- Save this switch immediately and show success/error feedback without changing ladder positions or challenge rules.

## Verification
- Confirm the switch renders in Club Ladder settings.
- Confirm an admin can switch member visibility off and on without losing access.
- Confirm the preview builds successfully.
