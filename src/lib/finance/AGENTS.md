# Finance rules (src/lib/finance)

- Member payment approval goes only through `finance_decide_member_transaction` (club-scoped finance permission, row lock, pending-only, audit row); why: Treasurers can approve without admin rights and a payment can never be posted twice.
- Finance Enter Transaction account choices come only from `src/lib/finance/transaction-accounts.ts`: Income and Payments out both list their P&L accounts plus all standard/club balance-sheet accounts, never the money accounts, Accounts Receivable or cost of sales; why: money in/out often settles balance-sheet items and posting is direction-agnostic.
- Standalone club-made Asset accounts (no base account, e.g. staff loans) carry on the `other_assets` ledger account, never `cash`/`bank_current`; why: a loan paid by EFT must reduce Bank without appearing as Cash.
- Finance statement downloads (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance) go through `src/lib/finance/report-export.ts` (one report model → CSV, Excel, PDF holding only club, title, period and report lines); why: printing the page leaks navigation and the three formats must never disagree.
- Card/cash/online bar purchases appear on both the member's My Account statement and the admin Member Statement only via `src/lib/finance/direct-bar-lines.ts` fed by `my_bar_purchases` (own member, club admin or Finance permission); they net to zero and never change GL balances; why: both statements must always list the same lines.
- Manual journals post only via `admin_post_journal` (finance writers, chairman view-only, balanced, member required exactly on debtors/member_credits lines, idempotent request id, audited); member credit transfers live in `member_credit_transfers` and move credit only when the receiver accepts via `respond_member_credit_transfer` (member_credits first then debtors from sender, receiver auto-settled), pure form rules in `src/lib/finance/manual-journal.ts`; why: no unbalanced or unattributed member postings and credit never moves without the receiver's consent.
