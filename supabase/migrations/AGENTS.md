# Tournament database rules (entry fees and doubles pairs)

- Cancelling/declining/withdrawing a tournament entry before `registration_closes_at` (else start date; never once entries are locked or for keep-position withdrawals) removes its unpaid entry fee via DB trigger `trg_registration_cancel_unpaid_fee` → `_champ_fee_adjust(fee, 0)` with audit; fees with any payment, credit, invoice or another live entry are never touched; why: invitations/cancelled entries must not leave debt, and after closing the fee stands.
- Doubles pairs re-settle when a registration's fee row turns paid (trigger `champ_fee_paid_settles_pairs`) as well as on registration payment, and payer totals count a covered partner only by that doubles event (`champ_member_event_paid`); why: settlement order left fully paid pairs on awaiting payment and showed payers a partner fee they had already paid.
- A doubles chooser may pick any invited, category-eligible player who has not entered that category yet (`list_doubles_partner_options.needs_entry`); `propose_doubles_partner` then adds the category to the partner's entry, reprices it via `refresh_tournament_entry_fee` after the pair exists (chooser cover counted, paid fees get an 'additional events' charge) and writes an audit event; why: a partner should not have to enter a category before being chosen for it.
- Tournament fee lines are named by DB `champ_fee_line_label`/`champ_reg_fee_label` ("<Tournament> – <Player> – <Category>"; extra categories "… – <Player> – additional categories", legacy "entry fee – additional events" still matched); invite-link acceptance charges through `ensure_tournament_entry_fee` + `refresh_tournament_entry_fee`, never a flat fee; why: shared family accounts must see whose entry each line is, and every path must price the package and partner covers the same way.
